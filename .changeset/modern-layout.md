New design: the app opens straight into a full-screen workbench – layers on the left, a live chart canvas in the middle, climate and result on the right; on phones the canvas sits on top of a tabbed bottom sheet. Templates and saved build-ups are in a searchable library (⌘K / Ctrl+K) instead of a start page. Light and dark mode follow the device setting, and the app has a new icon. Templates now live at `/<id>` and build-ups saved in this browser at `/local/<id>`; old `/calculator/<id>` links redirect.
